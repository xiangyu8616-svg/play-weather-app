中文

1. 我们收集的数据

  • 账号信息（可选）：如果你选择登录，我们通过 Supabase Auth 收集你的电子邮箱地址，仅用于登录验证。
  • 收藏地点：你收藏的城市列表。未登录时仅保存在本设备；登录后会同步到你的账号。
  • 设置偏好：语言、通知开关等，保存在本设备。
  • 天气查询：你查询的城市 ID 会发送给和风天气 API 以返回天气数据。我们不收集你的精确地理位置——本 App 不申请定位权限，所有地点由你手动选择。

2. 第三方服务

  • 和风天气 QWeather：天气数据来源，接收你查询的城市 ID。
  • NOAA SWPC：极光 Kp 指数数据，不发送任何个人信息。
  • Supabase：账号与收藏同步的后端服务（数据处理地：美国）。
  • Vercel：网站托管，可能记录标准访问日志（IP、User-Agent）。

3. 数据的保存与删除

本地数据（未登录状态的收藏、设置）仅保存在你的设备上，卸载 App 即删除。账号数据（邮箱、云端收藏）保存在 Supabase，直到你删除账号。

4. 删除账号

你可以在 App 内「我的 → 关于 → 删除账号」发起删除，或直接发送邮件至 xiangyu.8616@gmail.com， 邮件中注明注册邮箱。我们将在 7 天内删除你的账号及全部云端数据（邮箱、资料、收藏记录），并回复确认。删除不可撤销。

5. 联系我们 / 举报

隐私相关问题、内容举报或其他诉求，请邮件联系： xiangyu.8616@gmail.com。我们将在 7 天内回复。

6. 政策变更

本政策更新时会修改顶部日期并在此页面公示。重大变更会在 App 内提示。

English

1. Data We Collect

  • Account info (optional): if you sign in, we collect your email address via Supabase Auth, used solely for authentication.
  • Saved locations: your list of saved cities. Stored on-device when signed out; synced to your account when signed in.
  • Preferences: language and notification settings, stored on-device.
  • Weather queries: the city IDs you look up are sent to the QWeather API to fetch weather data. We do not collect your precise geolocation — the app never requests location permission; all places are chosen manually by you.

2. Third-Party Services

  • QWeather: weather data provider; receives the city IDs you query.
  • NOAA SWPC: aurora Kp-index data; no personal information is sent.
  • Supabase: backend for accounts and favorites sync (data processed in the US).
  • Vercel: hosting provider; may keep standard access logs (IP, User-Agent).

3. Retention & Deletion

On-device data (saved cities and settings while signed out) stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Account data (email, synced favorites) is kept in Supabase until you delete your account.

4. Account Deletion

You can request deletion in the app via Profile → About → Delete Account, or simply email xiangyu.8616@gmail.com from (or stating) your registered address. We will delete your account and all cloud data (email, profile, saved locations) within 7 days and confirm by reply. Deletion is irreversible.

5. Contact & Reporting

For privacy questions, content reports, or any other request, email xiangyu.8616@gmail.com. We respond within 7 days.

6. Changes

Updates to this policy are posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes will also be announced in-app.
